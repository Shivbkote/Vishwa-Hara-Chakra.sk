About / Vishwa Hara Chakra

A circular idea,
engineered into reality.

Vishwa Hara Chakra is positioned around a simple reversal: waste should not be the end of a material's life. It can become the beginning of the next useful system.

Our philosophy

Design the loop.

The name Vishwa Hara Chakra — विश्व हरा चक्र — evokes a “World's Green Revolution”: a wheel where resources circulate instead of being consumed once and discarded.

What we bring together

Materials. Energy. Thermal performance.

01

Circular materials

Industrial residues, fly ash, slag, silica fume and reclaimed construction & demolition waste become inputs to material systems.

02

Waste-to-energy

Anaerobic digestion converts suitable agricultural and waste streams into compressed Bio-CNG.

03

Thermal efficiency

Zephyr is positioned as an ultra-light, particle-infused nanofabric for reducing heat transfer across multiple applications.

04

Deployment thinking

The current site content emphasizes field deployment, testing and specification rather than presenting the work as only a prototype.

The operating model

From resource problem
to infrastructure solution.

01

03
