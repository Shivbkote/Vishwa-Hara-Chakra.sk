The idea

A wheel, run in reverse.

विश्व हरा चक्र
Vishwa Hara Chakra translates to “World's Green Revolution.” Instead of raw material becoming waste, we design systems where waste becomes raw material again.

01 — Core technologies

Three problems.
Engineered shut.

Materials, energy and thermal efficiency brought together as practical deployment pathways.

MAT—01

Cement-Free Concrete

Bricks, blocks and paving built without cement, using industrial and construction residues as feedstock.

View material system →
ENR—02

C&D Waste → Bio-CNG

Anaerobic digestion pathway for agricultural and construction-linked waste, producing compressed Bio-CNG.

View energy system →
INS—03

Zephyr Insulation

Particle-infused nanofabric designed to reduce heat transfer across walls, piping, tanks and vehicles.

View insulation system →
